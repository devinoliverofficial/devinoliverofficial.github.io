Greenroom Support

Last updated October 19, 2026

Greenroom is a tour app for artists, crew, and the people who follow them. This page says how to get help and how to do the things people ask about most.

Contact us

Email greenroom.tourbook@gmail.com. Tell us the email address on your account and what you were doing when it went wrong. We read every message and answer as soon as we can.

Signing in

  • Greenroom is in a closed beta. You need a Greenroom code, or an invitation to a tour, to make an account.
  • If you forgot your password, tap Forgot password on the sign-in screen and we will email you a way back in.

Delete your account

  • Open the menu (the three lines at the top right), tap Settings, then tap Delete my account.
  • This deletes your profile, messages, flowers, follows, and settings for good, and the tours you made and had deleted. Our Privacy Policy lists what is deleted and what can remain.
  • A tour you still run has to be deleted first. An artist page is kept as a permanent record, so an account that runs one stays until you email us.
  • If you cannot sign in, email us from the address on your account and we will help.

Disconnect a bank card

  • In Greenroom, open Settings, then Linked Cards, then tap the trash next to a bank, or tap Disconnect every bank.
  • Or go to my.plaid.com, where you can see and remove the apps connected to your banks.

Report a problem or abuse

  • To report a message, a tour chat line, a note that came with flowers, a guest name, a profile, or an artist page, tap Report where you see it. To stop hearing from a person, open their profile and tap Block. We look at every report within 24 hours.
  • For anything else that is wrong, email us.

Turn alerts on or off

Open Settings, then Notifications.

Fix or take down show history, an artist page, or a venue page

If something about an artist, a venue, or a show is wrong, or you are the artist, the venue, or the person it is about and want it corrected or taken down, email us and tell us what it is and where you saw it. Section 6 of our Privacy Policy says where show history comes from.
